# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 1, 2025  
**App Name:** Clinical Trial Navigator

---

## Overview

Clinical Trial Navigator is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share your data. By using the app, you agree to the terms described here.

---

## Data We Collect

We only collect health-related data that you explicitly allow through Apple HealthKit, including:

- Medications  
- Medical Conditions  
- Allergies  
- Age  
- Weight  
- Height

This data remains on your device and is only accessed when you use the app to search for clinical trials.

---

## How We Use Your Data

Your HealthKit data is used solely to:
- Personalize your clinical trial search results  
- Improve relevance of trial matches using AI  

To achieve this, we may share your anonymized data with:

- **ClinicalTrials.gov** (for matching you with appropriate trials)  
- **Google Gemini AI** (for generating summaries and recommendations)

We do **not** collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location.

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell or rent your data.  
We only share data with:
- **ClinicalTrials.gov** — to find matching clinical trials  
- **Google Gemini AI** — to help interpret and summarize trials

All shared data is de-identified and used solely for improving the app’s functionality.

---

## Data Storage

- All data is processed locally on your device.  
- No personal health data is stored on external servers.  
- We do not use tracking technologies or third-party analytics.

---

## Your Rights

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time from your iOS Settings.  
You can delete the app at any time to stop data collection.
